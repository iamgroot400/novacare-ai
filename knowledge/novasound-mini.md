# NovaSound Mini Troubleshooting

## No sound / very quiet

1. Raise volume on both the speaker and the phone.
2. Disconnect other Bluetooth audio devices nearby.
3. Power cycle the speaker (hold power for 5 seconds to turn off, then on).
4. Re-pair: hold the Bluetooth button until it double-beeps, then select
   **NovaSound Mini**.

## Speaker won't power on

- Charge for 30 minutes with a 5V USB-C charger, then try again.
- Check the USB-C port for lint or damage.
- If it never lights up after charging, check warranty and offer a support ticket.

## Distortion at high volume

Some distortion near maximum volume is normal for a speaker this size. Backing off
2–3 steps from maximum usually clears it. Persistent crackle at moderate volume may
be a driver fault — check warranty.

## Water exposure

NovaSound Mini is rated **IPX6** (strong water jets), not for submersion. If it was
dunked, dry it fully for 24 hours before charging. Water damage on submersion is not
covered by warranty.

## Pairing to two phones

NovaSound Mini remembers the last two devices. To switch, disconnect on the active
phone and connect from the other.
