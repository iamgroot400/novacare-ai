# NovaMouse Air Troubleshooting

## Mouse not connecting

1. Slide the power switch on the base to **ON** (green dot visible).
2. Charge via USB-C for 10 minutes.
3. Hold the mode button under the mouse for 3 seconds to enter Bluetooth pairing;
   the LED flashes fast.
4. Remove old "NovaMouse Air" entries on the computer and pair fresh.
5. To use the 2.4 GHz dongle instead, plug it in and short-press the mode button.

## Cursor stutters or jumps

- Use on an opaque, non-glossy surface; glass and mirrors confuse the sensor.
- Keep the receiver within 2 m, away from USB 3.0 ports and hubs (they cause 2.4 GHz
  interference) — use a short extension cable.
- Replace the surface or add a mouse pad.

## Double-clicking when clicking once

1. Update firmware in the NovaStore app.
2. Increase click debounce to "high".
3. If single clicks still double-register after a firmware update, this is a switch
   fault covered by the 12-month warranty — offer a support ticket.

## Battery drains quickly

Turn the mouse off when travelling (the switch, not just sleep). A full charge should
last several weeks of normal use. If it drops in days, check warranty.

## Scroll wheel skipping

Blow out dust around the wheel. If it still skips or scrolls the wrong direction
intermittently, open a support ticket.
