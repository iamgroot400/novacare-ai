# NovaPods Lite Troubleshooting

## Earbuds not appearing in the Bluetooth list

1. Put both earbuds in the case, lid open.
2. Hold the case button for **6 seconds** until the LED blinks.
3. Forget any old "NovaPods Lite" entry on your phone.
4. Toggle phone Bluetooth off and on.
5. Select **NovaPods Lite** from the list of new devices.
6. Update firmware to **1.8 or newer** in the NovaStore app — this fixes most early
   pairing problems.

## Choppy audio or stutter

- Keep the phone within 3 metres and in line of sight during setup.
- Disable battery optimisation for the NovaStore / Bluetooth service on Android.
- Turn off "dual audio" / "share audio" features on the phone.

## Weak ANC

NovaPods Lite ANC is lighter than NovaPods Pro. Ensure a firm ear-tip seal and that
ANC is toggled on. If the customer expects Pro-level cancellation, explain the
difference and, if within 14 days of delivery, a return may be possible.

## Short battery life

1. Fully charge the case for 90 minutes.
2. Do a full discharge / recharge cycle once.
3. If total listening time is still far below 35 hours combined, check warranty and
   offer a support ticket (battery failure outside normal degradation is covered).
