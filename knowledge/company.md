# About NovaStore

NovaStore is a **fictional** Nepal-based electronics e-commerce company used for the
NovaCare AI demonstration. Every product, order, customer and transaction in this
system is synthetic. No real payments are processed and no real money moves.

## What we sell

Audio (earbuds, speakers, soundbars), wearables (smartwatches and fitness bands),
computing accessories (keyboards, mice, webcams, docks, microphones) and charging
accessories. All products are Nova-branded.

## Support hours

NovaCare AI is available 24/7 for chat and in-browser voice calls. Human specialists
are available Sunday to Friday, 9:00–18:00 Nepal time. Complex hardware cases are
escalated to a human specialist.

## Contact

- Support: through NovaCare AI on the website (chat or "Call AI Support")
- Registered office: Kathmandu, Nepal (demo address)

## Payment methods (demo only)

Khalti, eSewa, IME Pay, ConnectIPS and Cash on Delivery. In this demo, selecting a
payment method never triggers a real charge.
