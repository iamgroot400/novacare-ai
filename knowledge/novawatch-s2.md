# NovaWatch S2 Troubleshooting

## Watch won't pair with the phone

1. Install the latest **NovaStore** app and sign in.
2. On the watch: Settings → System → Reset → keep data.
3. Keep the watch on its charger during pairing.
4. In the app: Devices → Add → NovaWatch S2, and accept the pairing code on both screens.
5. Make sure phone Bluetooth and Location permission are granted to the app.

## Inaccurate heart-rate or SpO2 readings

- Wear the watch snugly, one finger-width above the wrist bone.
- Clean the sensor glass on the back.
- Stay still for SpO2 spot checks.
- Cold skin lowers readings — warm up first.

## Poor battery life

1. Update watch firmware in the app.
2. Turn off always-on display and continuous SpO2.
3. Reduce workout GPS auto-detection.
4. If battery still drains in under a day with light use, check warranty and offer a
   support ticket.

## GPS won't lock

- Do the first fix outdoors with a clear sky view; allow up to 2 minutes.
- Sync the app while on Wi-Fi so it can download assisted-GPS data.

## Charging problems

Use the magnetic puck that shipped in the box. Align the pins to the contacts. Clean
both surfaces. If it will not charge with a known-good cable and adapter, this is a
hardware fault — check warranty and offer a support ticket.
