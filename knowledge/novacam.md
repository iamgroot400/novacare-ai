# NovaCam 2K Troubleshooting

## Camera not detected

1. Plug directly into a USB-C or USB-A port on the computer, not a hub.
2. Try a different cable — some charge-only cables carry no data.
3. On Windows: Settings → Privacy → Camera → allow desktop apps.
   On macOS: System Settings → Privacy & Security → Camera.
4. Close other apps that may hold the camera (Zoom, Teams, browser tabs).
5. Slide the privacy shutter fully open.

## Blurry or constantly refocusing image

- Clean the lens with a microfibre cloth.
- Give the scene more light; autofocus hunts in dim rooms.
- In the NovaCam app, switch to **fixed focus** and set it once.

## No microphone audio

1. In the app / OS sound settings, select **NovaCam 2K** as the input device.
2. Raise the input level and disable "exclusive mode" on Windows.
3. If the dual mics produce no signal at all in any app, this is a hardware fault —
   check warranty and offer a support ticket.

## Green or pink tint

Disable auto white balance and set it manually, or reset image settings to default
in the NovaCam app.

## Low frame rate at 2K

2K/30 needs a USB 3.0 port and adequate CPU. Drop to 1080p for smoother video on
older machines.
