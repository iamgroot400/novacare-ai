# Warranty Policy

## Coverage length

- Standard Nova electronics: **12 months** from the delivery date.
- NovaCharge chargers: **6 months** from the delivery date.

The exact `warranty_months` for a product is on its product record.

## What the warranty covers

- Manufacturing defects
- Charging failures not caused by physical damage
- Connectivity hardware problems (Bluetooth radio, pairing hardware faults)
- Display failures (dead pixels, lines, backlight failure)
- Battery failures outside normal degradation

## What the warranty does not cover

- Accidental physical damage (drops, cracks, crushed cases)
- Water damage on a product that is not rated for it
- Unauthorised modifications or repairs
- Lost or stolen products
- Normal battery capacity loss over time

## How warranty claims work in this MVP

Warranty claims **create a support ticket** (`SUP-XXXX`) for a specialist to review.
The MVP does not automatically dispatch replacements. If an item is within its
warranty period and the reported fault matches a covered category, NovaCare should
offer to create a support ticket after the customer confirms.

## Warranty vs return

A return is about the 14-day window (see `returns.md`). Warranty is about defects for
the full coverage length. An item can be **outside** the return window but **inside**
warranty — in that case, offer a support ticket, not a return.
