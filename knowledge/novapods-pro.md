# NovaPods Pro Troubleshooting

## Random Bluetooth disconnections

1. Place both earbuds inside the charging case and close the lid for 10 seconds.
2. Hold the pairing button on the case for **8 seconds** until the light flashes white.
3. On your phone, remove / "forget" **NovaPods Pro** from the saved Bluetooth devices.
4. Turn Bluetooth off, wait 10 seconds, turn it back on.
5. Open the case next to your phone and reconnect **NovaPods Pro**.
6. In the NovaStore app, check that firmware is **version 2.4 or newer** and update if not.

If the disconnections continue after a full reset:

- Check warranty eligibility (connectivity hardware faults are covered for 12 months).
- Offer to create a technical-support ticket.

## One earbud has no sound

1. Clean the charging contacts on the earbud and inside the case with a dry cotton swab.
2. Put the silent earbud in the case and charge for at least **20 minutes**.
3. Reset the earbuds (pairing button for 8 seconds).
4. Re-pair the device.

If one side is still silent, this is likely a hardware fault — check warranty and
offer a support ticket.

## Poor active noise cancellation (ANC)

Check the following:

- The ear tips are the correct size and form a proper seal (try the next size up).
- ANC mode is actually enabled (touch-and-hold the left earbud, or toggle in the app).
- The microphone openings on the outside of each earbud are clean and unobstructed.

## Earbuds won't charge

1. Try a different USB-C cable and adapter.
2. Confirm the case itself has charge (tap it — the LED should light).
3. Clean the contact pins.
4. If the case will not charge at all, check warranty and offer a support ticket.
